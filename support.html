Support

Perimeter is a sci-fi arena defense game — hold the generator against a small, smart raider squad across escalating waves. Need help, found a bug, or have a suggestion? We'd like to hear from you.

Fastest way to reach us: email nsuemml@gmail.com — include your device model and iOS version if you're reporting a bug, plus what you expected to happen, and we'll get back to you as soon as we can.

Common questions

  • Why do enemies ignore me and attack the generator? That's the core mechanic, not a bug — the raider squad prioritizes the generator over you whenever it can reach it. Position yourself to block their path in.
  • How do upgrades work? After each wave you pick one of three upgrade cards. Runs are short (6–10 minutes), so experiment freely between attempts.
  • Where is my progress saved? Locally on your device only — there's no account and no cloud sync. See our Privacy Policy for details.
  • Does this game need internet? No — Perimeter works fully offline.
  • How do I reset my progress? Settings → "Reset all progress."
